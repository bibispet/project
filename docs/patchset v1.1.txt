## A) Patchset Summary

Blueprint v1 is **patchable** (no full rebuild required) because its step structure, “thick client” posture, and most physics constraints already align with the Technical Appendix. The biggest risks are a handful of **high-impact omissions/contradictions** that will cause implementation drift: (1) a **wrong Lens Toggle order** in `docs/project_rules.md` vs the LOCKED Appendix spec, (2) a **missing Add Relative Modal step** (LOCKED §5.2) and its write-path endpoints, (3) the **Read Path Gap** (no explicit `ApiVaultRepo` adapter + memory download/decrypt path), and (4) **timeline ordering token ambiguity** (must be fractional indexing and must not encode dates). Patchset v1.1 below fixes these without rewriting the whole blueprint.
